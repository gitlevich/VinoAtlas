How this works

On each measure the shaded stretch is the range you accept. Only wines inside every range are shown, and the list is ordered by nearness to the middle of your ranges. Drag an end to narrow it, drag the middle to slide it, double-click to accept everything again. Telling the sommelier the same thing moves these ranges; dragging them is the same statement made by hand.

Ten years of a wine drinker, left to right

Where it is likely to be wrong

Nobody tasted any of this. The five numbers for each wine come from written descriptions of it, so where a description is wrong the wine lands in the wrong place — Port and Madeira are both filed wrongly, and there will be others. Oak is the shakiest of the five: no tasting panel ever tested whether people agree about it, it is here because the shop's list records it. And the order the ten orders appear in is inferred, not dated.
