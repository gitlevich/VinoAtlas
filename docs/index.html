VinoAtlas

Two tools, each reading one domain through the handful of measures its drinkers and growers actually agree on — and then finding what is near you in that space.

Wine

Cellar Compass

One buyer's account, read as a range on five measures — body, tannin grip, oak, fruit character, age — and the shop's 1,652 wines ranked by nearness to it. Talk to a sommelier that moves the page as you speak.

Open Weed

From Where You Stand

The same reading applied to cannabis, as a place you stand inside. Every smell and every reported effect is a direction; face the state you want and see which strains lie that way, and how far short of a promise the answer falls.

Open

Nobody tasted any of this. Each tool places things by what has been written about them, so where a description is wrong the thing lands in the wrong place. Every page says where it is likely to be wrong.
