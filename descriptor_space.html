The wine descriptor space

3,738 descriptor terms from 1.05M tasting notes, placed by how they are used. Two independent geometries — general semantics and in-domain co-occurrence — projected to two dimensions. Colour shows whether a word keeps the same company when you move between communities that never read each other.

How stability is measured

Each corpus gets its own co-occurrence geometry, computed separately. A term's neighbourhood is its 25 strongest co-occurrence partners in that corpus. Stability is the overlap of those neighbourhoods between corpora, then corrected for the frequency trend (raw stability correlates with log frequency at +0.24). It never asks whether people agree about a wine — only whether the word does the same job in each place.
